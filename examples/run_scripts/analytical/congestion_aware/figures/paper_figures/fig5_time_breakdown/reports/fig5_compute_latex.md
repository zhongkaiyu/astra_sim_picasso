# Fig 5a Compute Breakdown -- LaTeX Report

Fig.~5(a) decomposes per-layer decode latency into Proj QKV, Attention, Proj O, and Communication across all five platforms. At seq=8K with bs=1, our architecture achieves a total wall time of only 3.4\,$\mu$s, compared to 6.2\,$\mu$s for Rubin and 40.6\,$\mu$s for H100, an 11.8$\times$ reduction. This advantage stems from three compounding factors. First, the 16-NPU design provides an aggregate HBM bandwidth of 40\,TB/s (16$\times$2.5\,TB/s), 11.9$\times$ that of a single H100 (3.35\,TB/s). Since decode-phase GEMM operations are strictly memory-bandwidth-bound, our QKV latency is only 1,419\,ns versus 16,408\,ns on H100. Second, distributing attention heads across 16 NPUs reduces per-NPU KV cache volume by 16$\times$, lowering attention latency from 9,937\,ns (H100) to 913\,ns. Third, our RO\_new communication scheme adds merely 263\,ns of constant overhead, comprising QKV AllGather (76\,ns), Attention ReduceScatter (76\,ns), and Final Reduce (111\,ns), constituting less than 8\% of wall time. By contrast, H100 TP2 and Rubin TP2 incur $\sim$1,800\,ns NVLink AllReduce per layer even with only 2-way parallelism. As sequence length scales to 128K, our advantage persists (5.9 vs.\ 84.5\,$\mu$s, 14.4$\times$), because the bandwidth advantage equally accelerates the linearly growing attention component.
